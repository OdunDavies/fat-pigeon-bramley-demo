Coach-house bakery · Broad Lane

Come before eleven. The fat pigeons already have.

Ruth Diskin bakes sourdough, croissants, danishes and seasonal cakes in a converted 19th-century coach house behind 406 Broad Lane. Open Saturday mornings, a couple of doors from Bramley Baths.

Named for the well-loved fat pigeons in the garden. Two giant pigeon sculptures by Nat of Pearly Rubble now live out front.

What leaves the oven

Bread first. Then the things people queue for.

The counter changes with the week and the season. Ruth has said she uses Yorkshire flour where she can, especially for the bread, and keeps the menu small enough to bake herself.

Sourdough

Weekend loaves, including specials such as roast potato and rosemary when it is on. Less white flour than a supermarket loaf, baked for the Saturday counter.

Pastries

Croissants and danishes. Neighbours have singled out the egg custard tart, and cheese and leek danish when it appears.

Seasonal bits

Cakes and short-run bakes. Rhubarb and asparagus when they are in season, with cheese, milk and butter from local suppliers.

How a Saturday works

Three hours. Then the gate closes.

West Leeds Dispatch and The Hoot both record Saturday hours of 9am–12pm. Ruth has advised getting there before 11, when the queue is usually kinder.

  1. Check Instagram the day before. Special loaves and what sold last week live on @fat_pigeon_bakery, not on a fixed menu.
  2. Find the A-frame on Broad Lane. The bakery is the coach house in the yard, a few doors from Bramley Baths and across from the park.
  3. Coffee is next door in the garden. Bramley Batch pours batch brews on site. Their Instagram is @bramley.batch.
  4. Parkrun crowd knows the drill. Bramley parkrun regulars often stop for breakfast after the run.

The yard

Built in the back garden, on purpose.

In 2023 Ruth Diskin converted a 19th-century coach house behind her Broad Lane house into Fat Pigeon. She had been baking at the Mill Kitchen in Farsley, and told West Leeds Dispatch the name comes from the pigeons in the garden.

She is part of the Yorkshire Grain Alliance, which works with bakers, millers and farmers on whole grains. Flour for the bread has been described as coming especially from Stringers in East Yorkshire. Cheese from a Bramley supplier, asparagus from Whiteley’s, rhubarb in season, and organic milk and butter.

Fat Pigeon Bakery Ltd (company 15060033) is registered at this address. Ruth Mary Diskin is the person with significant control.

Good to know

Prices are not published in a fixed list. The 2024 bread subscription — a Friday loaf for four weeks — was offered by email. Whether that is running now is something to ask directly.

Gluten-free bakes have been asked about on Instagram. Nothing public confirms a free-from range, so check before you travel if that is essential.

Questions people already ask

Before you cross Leeds for a croissant.

Are you only open on Saturdays?

Published hours are Saturdays, 9am–12pm (West Leeds Dispatch, updated November 2025, and The Hoot, June 2026). An earlier 2023 report said 10am–12pm; the later articles corrected that.

Where exactly is the door?

406 Broad Lane, LS13 3DF. The bakery is the coach house in the back yard. An A-frame on the path marks the gate.

Can I reserve a loaf?

There is no public booking page. Email fatpigeonbakery@gmail.com or message @fat_pigeon_bakery. A four-week Friday bread subscription was offered in September 2024; confirm if it is still running.

Who is in the garden besides the pigeons?

Bramley Batch serves coffee in the garden. The pigeon sculptures are by Nat of Pearly Rubble.
